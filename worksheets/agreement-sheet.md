# Shared signal agreement

Print this sheet or copy it into your own notes. It records a human agreement; the practice page does not save or read it. Use invented choices first, before trying the agreement in a game.

Add rows for your actual set. Do not leave an unused signal with two competing meanings. A stop signal must be distinguishable from an ordinary message in the agreement you choose.

## Test before separating

1. The sender privately chooses one practice item and signals it.
2. The receiver states or indicates the choice they understood, without acting on the game.
3. The sender confirms or stops the exchange.
4. Swap roles. Test a different choice and the stop signal.
5. If the views disagree, change the agreement and repeat the small test.

This exercise checks whether two people interpret a code alike. It does not establish that every signal is visible, accessible, or suitable for a particular puzzle. Return to the [hosted practice page](https://szj2ys.github.io/co-op-signal-practice/) for random sender cards and readback trials, or use the [observation sheet](observation-sheet.md) to record a mismatch.
